Ready for the later IMU work

Visual estimates provide the current replay poses. A future visual-inertial estimator can supply position and orientation through the same pose interface.

The IMU can help constrain orientation and gravity and support motion estimation between images. Camera/IMU timestamps, units and mounting must be calibrated first. This viewer has no live IMU data and does not integrate raw acceleration into position.
