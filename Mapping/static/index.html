Recording rate is checked when the Pi connects.

Higher rates save more overlapping views and use more storage. Shorter exposure reduces motion smear but may darken the image. Apply between recordings; Standard restores automatic exposure.

How to get a useful first scan
  1. Remove the props and keep the Pi powered while carrying the drone.
  2. Use a bright, textured room. Walk slowly with the camera facing furniture and walls.
  3. Move sideways and forward; keep successive views overlapping. Turning in place is not enough.
  4. Walk for 60–120 seconds, return toward the start, then stop and save.

Start with an empty room. People moving through the image make reconstruction harder.
