What was tested

Three complete map revisions are sent in checked pieces. Only a complete revision replaces the visible map. Older revisions, wrong session/frame identities, damaged content and expired positions are rejected. Missing pieces are requested again; a newer map replaces queued older work.

Heartbeat and retry messages share the simulated bandwidth. A connection interruption keeps the last completed map visible. A heartbeat can show that the connection recovered; it cannot make an old camera observation fresh.

The producer schedule waits for each batch’s last image, then adds its measured warm model time: . Image transfer, preprocessing and alignment would add more delay. This is an optimistic schedule using saved results, not a live speed benchmark.

The link carries scenes already computed on the Mac to a hypothetical display receiver. It does not show the Pi producing AI maps, or solve the camera uplink. No people positions, IMU data, radio hardware or Quest connection are simulated.

All times share an exact simulation clock. Real deployment still requires clock alignment, authenticated transport and hardware measurements. The rates are assumed application bandwidth including the JSON messages, excluding radio framing and error correction.
